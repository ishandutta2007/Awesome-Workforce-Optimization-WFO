# Awesome-Workforce-Optimization-WFO

# Top Workforce Optimization (WFO) Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Workforce Management, Contact Center Scheduling & Quality Optimization*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Workforce Optimization (WFO)**. These tools enable organizations to forecast demand, schedule staff efficiently, monitor real-time adherence, and improve agent performance through coaching and quality management in contact centers and service operations.

**Examples** include ServiceNow Workforce Optimization, NICE CXone, Verint Workforce Optimization, Calabrio ONE, Genesys Cloud CX, Alvaria, Playvox, Talkdesk Workforce Management, Five9, and injixo (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with active projects for call center simulation, workforce scheduling algorithms, and staffing optimization — ideal for operations researchers, contact center engineers, and developers building transparent WFO tooling without per-seat licensing lock-in.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[ServiceNow Workforce Optimization](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  
  WFO suite integrated with CSM featuring manager dashboards, channel management, AI-assisted scheduling, coaching modules, and skills-based routing for service teams .

- **[NICE CXone](https://www.nice.com/)**  
  Cloud-native WFM with AI-powered forecasting, machine learning scheduling, real-time intraday management, and agent self-service for omnichannel contact centers .

- **[Verint Workforce Optimization](https://www.verint.com/)**  
  Enterprise WFM platform with omni-channel demand forecasting, strategic capacity planning, automated scheduling, and the TimeFlex Bot for employee-driven schedule flexibility .

- **[Calabrio ONE](https://www.calabrio.com/)**  
  Integrated WFO suite combining quality management (QM), workforce management (WFM), and analytics with AI-powered Auto Coaching and intraday re-forecasting capabilities .

- **[Genesys Cloud CX](https://www.genesys.com/)**  
  Cloud contact center platform with built-in resource management covering forecasting, scheduling, shift trading, adherence monitoring, and service goal configuration .

- **[Alvaria](https://www.alvaria.com/)**  
  Workforce optimization suite with Real Time Optimizer for automated adherence management, dynamic training delivery during idle time, and intraday staffing adjustments .

- **[Playvox](https://www.playvox.com/)**  
  Workforce engagement management platform (now part of NICE) combining forecasting, conflict-free scheduling, real-time adherence visibility, and gamified agent recognition .

- **[Talkdesk Workforce Management](https://www.talkdesk.com/)**  
  WFM module with CSV forecast import, team-scoped permissions, bulk schedule uploads, queue tagging, and multi-queue forecast editing for resource planners .

- **[Five9](https://www.five9.com/)**  
  Workforce engagement management with agent-facing WFM tools for time-off planning, schedule viewing, forecast manipulation, and adherence reporting .

- **[injixo](https://www.injixo.com/)**  
  Cloud WFM platform with AI/ML-powered forecasting, automatic schedule optimization, absence management, and employee self-service; integrates with Zendesk and other helpdesks .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[pyworkforce](https://github.com/rodrigo-arenas/pyworkforce)**  
  Python library providing standard tools for workforce management including queuing theory, scheduling, rostering, and optimization problems; designed for call center and service operations planning .

- **[Callcenter Simulator](https://github.com/A-Herzog/Callcenter-Simulator)**  
  Free, platform-independent Java application for analyzing staffing requirements in call centers using event-oriented stochastic simulation to compute parameters beyond the Erlang C formula .

- **[Mini Callcenter Simulator](https://github.com/A-Herzog/Mini-Callcenter-Simulator)**  
  Lightweight Java simulator that models call center dynamics with parameters not covered by standard Erlang C calculations; useful for validating staffing models before deployment .

- **[pyworkforce alternatives ecosystem](https://relatedrepos.com/gh/rodrigo-arenas/pyworkforce)**  
  Community-curated listing of related Python packages for WFM, including forecasting, scheduling, and optimization libraries that complement pyworkforce .

- **Field management SaaS** — Open-source web-based job organization and field staff management solutions applicable to distributed workforce scheduling .
- **Genetic algorithm schedulers** — Community projects solving shift scheduling with availability constraints and personnel requirements using GA optimization .

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **pyworkforce** for forecasting and optimization, **Callcenter Simulator** for validating staffing models via stochastic simulation, and community scheduling libraries from the GitHub workforce-management topic for building a custom, self-hosted WFO pipeline focused on contact center operations.
